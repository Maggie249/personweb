Practical technology design

Proven components, clearly controlled.

The service integrates existing AI services, open frameworks and local Hong Kong content. It does not train an expensive custom model or build unnecessary hardware.

Agentic workflow

Connected tasks across job analysis, CV revision, interview practice and application tracking.

Localised RAG

Hong Kong job information, public services and hiring workflows form the knowledge base.

PII redaction

Personal identifiers are removed before content reaches the AI model.

Cantonese voice AI

Local-language voice practice for behavioural and role-specific interview questions.

Evidence checker

Generated claims are compared with the participant's verified experience and records.

Capability scoring

Prompting, verification, privacy, bias awareness and result application are scored as separate capabilities.

Verifiable badge

Completion is converted into a capability record that an employer can review.

Clear technology boundary. No custom large-model training, no new hardware requirement and no complex blockchain platform. Spend is focused on integration, local content and service quality.
